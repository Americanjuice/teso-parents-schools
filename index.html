About Us

Rooted in faith, led by the community

Teso Parents' Nursery and Primary Schools is a Christian school network serving 1,284 children and their families across Ngora, Katakwi and Soroti districts in Uganda's Teso region, with 22 teachers.

The schools are led by Founder and CEO Pastor Charles Dicken Olupot from Mukura Town Council in Ngora District. Alongside the classroom, the schools feed children, disciple them in faith, support teenage mothers, and teach families climate-smart farming so the community can sustain itself.

Our motto: “Trust God, Aim Higher”

We warmly welcome volunteers, individual donors, and church partners.

Programs

More than a classroom

Feeding Program

School meals so children can learn on a full stomach. Our goal is to feed 500 children for 12 months.

Discipleship

1,284 Bibles distributed, the very first Bible for most of our children, and active Bible clubs where children grow in faith and character.

Teenage Mothers

Care and support for teenage mothers in our communities, helping young mothers and their children build a hopeful future.

Smart School Gardens

Climate-smart agriculture: building low-cost greenhouses with drip irrigation to grow vegetables year-round while conserving water. Children and mothers learn organic farming to replace harmful chemicals.

Football Academy for Girls and Boys

Based at Teso Parents Nursery School – Mukura, our academy trains both girls and boys in football, discipline, teamwork, and Godly character, keeping youth in school and away from early marriage and idleness.

Dairy for the Children

Managed by Akello Florence, our school farm in Mukura maintains 3 dairy cows producing 20 litres of milk daily for 10 paying customers. It provides nutrition for our children, trains local farmers, and generates sustainable income for the school.

Hospital Compassion

Crafting and providing soft bonding hearts for premature babies in local hospitals.

Partnership Opportunities

Bring the Guthridge Writing System to your school

Through Raven Ink's white-label program, Pastor Charles trains teachers in the Guthridge Writing System (GWS) and Raven Ink. GWS teaches students to think and write using the hyperthesis method, one clear step at a time.

  • At a conference venue near you. Charles brings the training to a venue close to your school or district.
  • At Teso Parents' schools. Teachers come to Charles's schools to learn the system step by step in real classrooms.
  • Step-by-step training. Teachers learn the full GWS process and how to use Raven Ink with their students.

Raven Ink and the Guthridge Writing System are provided by Raven Ink (ravenink.io).
